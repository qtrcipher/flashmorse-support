Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

العربية

The short version: FlashMorse collects no data. None. Morse communication and processing work on your device without an account. Opening this policy loads this website.

Data collection

Morse Flashing collects no data. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data or usage data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs in the app.

Camera and microphone

FlashMorse asks for camera access to detect incoming Morse light flashes, and microphone access to detect incoming Morse tones. All processing happens on your device in real time. Camera frames and audio are analyzed instantly and never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Your messages

Messages stay on your phone unless you send them using light or sound, or share them. Saved templates and history are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this local data.

Purchases

The optional “FlashMorse Pro” upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase receipts are handled by Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Children

FlashMorse is safe for all ages. Since we collect no data at all, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hamam@gccapps.com.
